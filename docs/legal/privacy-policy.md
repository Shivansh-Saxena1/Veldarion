## 2. Scope of this Privacy Policy; roles and legal relationships

This Privacy Policy covers three distinct categories of information and it is important to understand the differences:

1. **Website and business information** — information we collect directly from visitors to veldarion.com, prospective customers, and customer representatives (Sections 4–5).
2. **Customer-provided information** — account information and documents that our business customers (clinics and their revenue-cycle teams) provide to obtain our services (Sections 6–7).
3. **Patient information** — personal and health information **about patients**, contained inside the documents our customers upload (Section 8).

For website and business information, Veldarion is the entity responsible for the information. For patient information contained in customer-uploaded documents, the **customer (the clinic) is the party responsible for that information and for its relationship with the patient**; Veldarion processes it on the customer's behalf under a written agreement (a Business Associate Agreement where the information is protected health information regulated by HIPAA). This Privacy Policy does not create a direct relationship between patients and Veldarion, and patients are not our customers.

## 7. Uploaded documents and clinical information

Customers upload documents relating to denied claims so that our platform can analyze them and prepare appeal drafts. These documents typically include insurance denial letters, EOBs, ERA/835 data, clinical notes, and patient records, and they ordinarily contain patient names, identification numbers, dates of birth, diagnoses, procedures, and claim financials. We process these documents solely to provide the services the customer has requested: extracting relevant information, analyzing denial reasons against payer policies and coverage criteria, identifying supporting evidence in the customer's own records, and generating structured appeal drafts for review by the customer's authorized personnel.

**Customers must upload only documents they are authorized to provide to us.** See Section 8 and our Terms of Service.

## 8. Protected Health Information (PHI)

Documents uploaded by customers will frequently contain Protected Health Information as defined by the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act ("HIPAA").

- Veldarion processes PHI **only on behalf of a customer that is a HIPAA Covered Entity (or another vendor acting for one)** and **only under a separately executed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement ("BAA")** between Veldarion and that customer. Website Terms of Service alone do not authorize the submission of PHI.
- **Customers must not upload documents containing PHI unless and until a BAA has been executed with Veldarion.** Our platform is intended to enforce this requirement administratively.
- Under the BAA, we use and disclose PHI only as needed to provide the services, we report security incidents and breaches to the customer, we flow HIPAA obligations down to subcontractors that handle PHI, and we return or destroy PHI at the end of the relationship in accordance with the BAA.
- We do not sell PHI, use PHI for marketing, or use PHI to train or fine-tune models for our own purposes.
- Patients whose information appears in customer-uploaded documents are not our customers. **Patients should direct privacy questions and rights requests to the clinic that treated them**; we support our customers in responding to those requests as required by the BAA and applicable law.
